    How scoring works

    Each card is a fragment from one of nine classic fantasy, adventure and horror novels. Every turn you are dealt five, drawn from near and far of where you stand. Play the one that best continues the meaning of your last few cards. The latest card counts most.

    A card that fits the thread raises harmony; one that breaks it lowers harmony. Consecutive good cards build a streak that multiplies gains up to double, and a bad card ends it. After each move you see what every card in the hand would have scored. Reach 100 in as few moves as you can. Careful play gets there in about ten.
